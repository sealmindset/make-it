# VEX

One statement per (product, vulnerability or non-CVE item). Store the list in `data/derived/vex.json`
(generated by the project's assess script from evidence); `scripts/openvex.py` renders OpenVEX v0.2.0 and refuses
invalid statements.

## Statuses (CISA minimum elements)
| Status | When | Must carry |
|---|---|---|
| `affected` | Evidence shows the product present at an affected version (or below the fixed build) | `action_statement` (what, who, when) |
| `not_affected` | Evidence covers **the place the component would live** and shows it absent / not exploitable | `justification` (one of: component_not_present, vulnerable_code_not_present, vulnerable_code_not_in_execute_path, vulnerable_code_cannot_be_controlled_by_adversary, inline_mitigations_already_exist) and `impact_statement` with the evidence scope and date |
| `fixed` | Evidence shows the fix applied (build ≥ fixed build) | the evidence |
| `under_investigation` | Anything else — including "probably present" and "probably absent" | `evidence` (what was seen), `action_statement` (the check that resolves it), `owner` |

Rules learned:
- Tenant evidence can prove absence only of things that live in the tenant (an OAuth app, a service principal).
  Appliances, on-prem servers, websites and devices stay `under_investigation` until their owner confirms — even with
  zero hints. Name hints raise priority; they never settle status.
- Split a product into separate statements when the evidence differs (servers measured vs PCs not measured;
  Windows vs Edge component; Windows Office vs Mac Office with its own CVE list).
- Code inventories: say what was scanned, the export date, that manifests ≠ deployed and indirect dependencies may
  be missing; developer tools (e.g. Visual Studio) are a separate product.
- Priority: "Now" for exploited/KEV items that are present or plausibly present; dates for the rest.
- Count statements, not table rows, in the briefing ("18 items; two rows combine two items").
